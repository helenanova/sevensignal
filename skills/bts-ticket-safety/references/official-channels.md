# Where official BTS announcements actually appear

Use this to answer "where do I check?" and to re-verify stale claims.

한국어 요약: 공식 정보는 아래 채널에만 올라옵니다. 다른 곳의 주장은 여기서
확인하기 전까지 미확인입니다.

## Announcements and tour notices
- `bts.ibighit.com` - tour pages and notices, including each tour's official
  seller list per region. This is the first place to check.
- `weverse.io` (BTS community) - official notices, presale registration windows,
  membership announcements.

## Membership and presale registration
- `shop.weverse.io` - Weverse Membership purchase and status. Presale
  registration is free after membership and happens only through flows linked
  from official notices.

## Corporate confirmations
- `hybecorp.com` - corporate announcements (tours, partnerships).

## How to verify a seller for a specific tour
1. Open the tour's page or notice on `bts.ibighit.com` or `weverse.io`.
2. Follow the ticket link from that notice - do not search for the seller.
3. The domain you land on must match `data/official-domains.yaml`. If the
   notice names a seller not yet in the allowlist, the allowlist needs an
   update (see CONTRIBUTING.md); until then the verdict is UNVERIFIED, and you
   say so.

## What official channels never do
- Never sell or confirm tickets by DM, email, or phone.
- Never ask for payment outside the seller's own checkout.
- Never ask for your password or full card number to "verify" an account.
