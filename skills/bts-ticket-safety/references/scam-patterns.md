# Ticket scam patterns

Pattern catalog for screening. One hit forces a SCAM_INDICATORS verdict.
Each pattern: what it looks like, why it works, red flags.

한국어 요약: 아래 패턴이 하나라도 해당하면 사기 징후(SCAM_INDICATORS) 판정입니다.

## P1. Typosquat or lookalike ticketing site
- Looks like: `interpark-tickets.com`, `ticketmaster-bts.net`, `weverse-ticket.shop`.
- Why it works: the page copies the real site's design.
- Red flags: domain fails allowlist matching; URL arrived by DM, ad, or search ad.

## P2. Social media "extra ticket" seller
- Looks like: "Selling 2 floor seats, can't go anymore" on X, Instagram, Discord, Reddit.
- Why it works: fan solidarity; the account looks like a real fan.
- Red flags: new or recently renamed account; refuses official-platform transfer;
  pushes the deal to DMs; urgency ("must sell today").

## P3. Unsafe payment rail
- Looks like: gift cards, wire transfer, crypto, Zelle, Venmo/PayPal
  "friends and family", cash app of any kind.
- Why it works: these payments are final and anonymous.
- Red flags: any payment method with no buyer protection. Official sellers take
  normal card payments on their own site, full stop.

## P4. Fake ticket-transfer screenshot
- Looks like: a screenshot of a "Ticketmaster transfer" email or app screen.
- Why it works: the screenshot looks official.
- Red flags: real transfers happen only inside the ticketing account, from the
  seller's account to the buyer's own account. A screenshot is proof of nothing.

## P5. Presale code for sale
- Looks like: "BTS presale code, $20, DM me."
- Why it works: fans panic about missing presale registration.
- Red flags: presale access comes from free Weverse membership registration or an
  official seller's own program. Codes are never sold.

## P6. QR / e-ticket resale
- Looks like: a screenshot of a QR code or PDF ticket offered for sale.
- Why it works: the QR looks real.
- Red flags: one QR can be sold to many buyers; only the first scanned enters.
  Many tours delay or bind QR delivery to the buyer account. No protection.

## P7. Account-verification phishing
- Looks like: "Verify your Ticketmaster/Weverse account before presale or lose
  your spot" with a link.
- Why it works: deadline fear before onsale.
- Red flags: login link not on an allowlisted domain; threats of losing access;
  asks for card details to "verify".

## P8. Impersonated fan-staff or "official helper"
- Looks like: a DM from "BTS ticketing support" or a fanbase admin offering
  "allocation tickets".
- Why it works: borrowed authority.
- Red flags: official staff never sell through DMs. Ever.

## P9. "Official resale" confusion
- Looks like: a marketplace calling its markup "official platinum" or
  "verified resale" at 3-10x face value.
- Why it works: some real sellers do run official resale, so the words sound right.
- Red flags: not named as the official resale channel for this tour by a Tier 1
  notice; price far above face value with pressure framing.

## P10. Fake customer support
- Looks like: search ads or posts with a "Ticketmaster support phone number".
- Why it works: fans with a problem search for help and call the scammer.
- Red flags: support contact reached anywhere except the official site's own
  help pages.
